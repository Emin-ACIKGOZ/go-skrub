--- NOTES ---

- Small struct performance is at parity with go-validator.
  The 28 vs 10 allocs comes from the ChainConfig wrapping layer.
- Deep matrix is 2.3x slower on skrub because SliceRule allocates
  per-call flyweights instead of caching them (safe for concurrency).
  Users who need max matrix perf can use the old SliceChain directly.
- Old CAS-guarded chains are faster but can't be shared across goroutines.
- New stateless rules are goroutine-safe with a small overhead.
- Core validators (URL, Email, IPv4) are very fast — no struct reflection.

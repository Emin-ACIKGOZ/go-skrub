--- ARCHITECTURE ---

Bind() is goroutine-safe via sync.Pool per-goroutine flyweight caching.
The first Validate() per goroutine allocates flyweights; subsequent calls
reuse via SetTarget() — zero allocs for elements after the first batch.

BindCAS() skips the pool and mutates flyweights directly via CAS guard.
Use for maximum single-goroutine throughput.
